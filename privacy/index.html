Kitty Max privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-16

Your data stays yours

Kitty Max keeps your cat, goals, check-ins, journal and progress on your iPhone. There is no Kitty Max account (Friends uses Sign in with Apple, only if you choose it), and we don't run servers that collect your data.

iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud sync, your data is stored in your own private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can read. Turning it off stops syncing; you can delete the iCloud copy from Settings.

Friends

If you sign in to Friends with Apple, your friends can see your cat's name, look and room, your bond and Max levels with the five attributes, this week's streak dots, the name you chose for friends, and the hugs, cheers and gifts you send them. They're stored in iCloud through Apple's sharing, readable only by people you invite. Your journal, moods, routines and Health data are never shared. Leave Friends (or Delete everything) removes your garden and friendships from every friend's device; gifts friends already received stay theirs. Hugs, gifts and new friends can notify you; turn that off in Friends settings. If you choose to share an experiment's finding with friends, they see what you tried and what changed, in words (for example "fell asleep 20 minutes earlier on average"). Nothing else from Health is ever shared, and you can stop sharing any time.

Weather

If you let your cat see the weather, Kitty Max uses your approximate location only to ask Apple Weather what it's like outside. The location isn't stored, synced or sent anywhere else, and the app works the same without it.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, Kitty Max reads sleep (including when you fell asleep and woke, and sleep stages), steps and walking distance, workouts, exercise and stand time, active energy, time in daylight, mindful minutes, heart rate variability, resting and walking heart rate, breathing rate while asleep, sleeping wrist temperature, blood oxygen and UV exposure where your watch records them, cardio fitness (VO2 max), and water and caffeine if another app logs them. It uses them only to tick your protocols, learn your baseline, show your scores, vitals and trends, suggest lighter days and judge your experiments. A finished experiment keeps only its verdict and the size of each change, never the daily numbers. The raw numbers stay on your phone: they're never stored by Kitty Max, never synced to iCloud and never sent anywhere. The one exception is Plus's monthly letter: it keeps one typical value per measure for the month (for example, your typical night's sleep) with your mail, on your phone and in your own iCloud if sync is on. Kitty Max writes only one thing to Health: your breathing and calm sessions, as mindful minutes. You can change access any time in the Health app.

Morning log

If you log bed and wake times by hand, or rate your skin on the Glow page, they're stored with the rest of your data on your phone (and in your own iCloud if sync is on), like your journal. If you turn on context for your age and sex on the Max sheet, the age range and table you pick stay on this phone only: never synced, counted or sent.

Purchases

Plus and yarn are bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we only receive a confirmation that a purchase happened, never your payment details.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your device. Nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Anonymous counts

To learn whether Kitty Max helps people keep going, each install adds 1 to a shared weekly tally the first time it reaches one of ten steps: installed, finished onboarding, ticked a first step, came back the next day, in week two or after a month, saw the Plus screen, started a free week, paid for Plus, or opened a share sheet. The tally is kept per app build and install week in Kitty Max's public iCloud database. It never includes your name, an ID, your device, your Health data, journal, goals or anything you wrote, and we never see who added to it. Uploads happen about once a day at a random time. Turn it off in Settings under Anonymous counts.

Analytics and ads

Kitty Max has no ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics.

Deleting your data

Settings ▸ Your data lets you export everything or delete it. Deleting the app removes the data on your phone.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or anything else: email goldenhourlabs.support@gmail.com. Report a problem (in Help & support) writes that email for you with your app version, iOS version, phone model, language, how many days you've had your cat, and whether Plus, iCloud sync and Apple Health are on. You see all of it before sending and can change or delete any of it. It never includes your name, journal, moods, plan or Health numbers, and nothing is sent unless you send the email.

Kitty Max offers wellness habits, not medical advice.
