Kitty Max privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-16

Your data stays yours

Kitty Max keeps your cat, goals, check-ins, journal and progress on your iPhone. There is no Kitty Max account (Friends uses Sign in with Apple, only if you choose it), and we don't run servers that collect your data.

iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud sync, your data is stored in your own private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can read. Turning it off stops syncing; you can delete the iCloud copy from Settings.

Friends

If you sign in to Friends with Apple, your friends can see your cat's name, look and room, your bond and Max levels with the five attributes, this week's streak dots, the name you chose for friends, and the hugs, cheers and gifts you send them. They're stored in iCloud through Apple's sharing, readable only by people you invite. Your journal, moods, routines and Health data are never shared. Leave Friends (or Delete everything) removes your garden and friendships from every friend's device; gifts friends already received stay theirs. Hugs, gifts and new friends can notify you; turn that off in Friends settings.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, Kitty Max reads sleep (including when you fell asleep and woke, and sleep stages), steps and walking distance, workouts, exercise and stand time, active energy, time in daylight, mindful minutes, heart rate variability, resting and walking heart rate, breathing rate while asleep, cardio fitness (VO2 max), and water and caffeine if another app logs them. It uses them only to tick your protocols, learn your normal, show your trends and suggest lighter days. The raw numbers stay on your phone: they're never stored by Kitty Max, never synced to iCloud and never sent anywhere. Kitty Max writes only one thing to Health: your breathing and calm sessions, as mindful minutes. You can change access any time in the Health app.

Morning log

If you log bed and wake times by hand, they're stored with the rest of your data on your phone (and in your own iCloud if sync is on), like your journal.

Purchases

Plus and yarn are bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we only receive a confirmation that a purchase happened, never your payment details.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your device. Nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Analytics and ads

Kitty Max has no ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics.

Deleting your data

Settings ▸ Your data lets you export everything or delete it. Deleting the app removes the data on your phone.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/gshorin-dev/kitty-max-legal, or write to the developer through the App Store listing.

Kitty Max offers wellness habits, not medical advice.
