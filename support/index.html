Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea? Email goldenhourlabs.support@gmail.com. We aim to reply within a few days. In the app, Help & support ▸ Report a problem adds the facts we need, like your app version and phone model, and you can read them first.

Most answers are in Help, the same articles as in the app.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone. With iCloud sync on, also in your own private iCloud. Settings ▸ Your data exports or deletes everything.

Cancel or restore Plus

Cancel any time in Settings ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions, at least a day before it renews. On a new phone, open Plus and tap Restore purchases. Anything you collected stays yours.

Apple Health

Health access is optional and can be changed in the Health app. Health data never leaves your phone.

Friends

Friends see your cat, its look and room, your levels and this week's streak, never your journal, moods, routines or Health data. Friends settings ▸ Leave Friends removes your garden from every friend's device.

Kitty Max offers wellness habits, not medical advice.
