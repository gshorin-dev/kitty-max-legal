Getting started

Why Kitty Max

A cat that grows with you, and your own numbers to show what's working.

A cat that grows with you. Small daily steps care for your cat. It grows, dresses up, explores and remembers what you've done together. Miss a day and nothing bad happens; your cat is never sad at you.

And your own numbers. Connect Apple Health and Kitty Max learns your usual: sleep, steps, heart rate. When you try a habit, it shows how your numbers look on days you did it, next to your own baseline. No scores against strangers, no generic advice.

What we never do. No guilt, no grades, no streak shaming. Your health numbers stay on your phone. We don't say a habit fixed anything; we show what changed since you started, and you decide what it means.

Coming from Finch

Keep your bird. Kitty Max adds the numbers Finch can't show, and it works fine beside it.

When you set up your cat, say you're coming from Finch. The fullest way is Finch's own export: in Finch, open Settings, Your Data, Advanced Management, Export Data, then pick the file. Or add screenshots of your goals and streak, or pick goals by hand. Everything is read on your phone. Check it, then carry on.

Your Finch streak carries on here: a 400-day streak becomes 401 on your first day of care. It shows as days with Finch, in your memory book and your history.

If you log how you feel in Finch or the Mindfulness app and connect Apple Health, Kitty Max reads that as your mood on days you don't check in here.

Creating your cat

Kitty Max starts by adopting a kitten. It takes about a minute.

  1. Pick a kitten and its coat.
  2. Choose its name and pronouns. You can change both later in your cat's tab.
  3. Choose what you want to max: sleep, light, movement, food, calm, focus, recovery or glow (skin, hair, teeth and posture).
  4. Set your starting line: your first numbers from Apple Health, or how you are today. Your Max sheet compares against it.
  5. Choose Gentle or Max mode. Max starts protocols a level up and levels up faster; change it in Settings.
  6. Accept the starter plan of small steps, then tick your first one on Home.

Your cat grows up with you. It stays kind at every age; its confidence grows as your bond does.

Your first two weeks

Kitty Max opens a little at a time, so you never meet everything at once. Your cat tells you each time something new opens.

  • Day 0: your cat, your plan, Health, the Shop, Calm & focus
  • Day 1: Quests, with daily quests and adventures
  • Day 3: the journal
  • Day 5: experiments, once any measure has a baseline (by day 14 at the latest)
  • Day 10: Patterns, journeys and discoveries
  • Day 14: Friends
  • Day 15: the Micropet Lab

The first seven quests are a short tour: tick one step, see one number next to your baseline, read why a protocol might help, dress your cat, start an experiment, look back in Patterns, and send a hug.

Exploring the Home screen
  • Your cat's room: its line sits under the art. Tap your cat to pet it.
  • The pills: adventure, streak, and your Max level, which opens Health.
  • Today's plan: your steps, coloured by area. Some tick themselves from Apple Health. A finished step slides away; "Done today" shows them again if you need to undo one.
  • Mornings: readiness and last night's sleep. Evenings: a wind-down, and "Today I…" in the journal.
  • The welcome week: seven small rewards in your first days of care.
  • Sundays: a short note looking back at your week.

The tabs along the bottom are Home, Health, Quests, Shop and your cat's own tab. Settings, your yarn and Mail are along the top.

Areas, protocols and steps

An area is something you can max: light, sleep, move, fuel, calm, focus, recover or glow. A protocol is a research-backed habit inside an area. A step is what shows on today's plan.

Each protocol has levels. You start tiny, and the level moves up when you do it often enough. It never moves down on its own, and you can make it lighter any time. Every protocol lists its sources.

To change your plan, open My steps from the Health tab. Step ideas there are starting points you can edit.

Yarn, bond and Max level

Yarn is what care earns. You spend it in the Shop. Bond is how close you and your cat are; heart moments arrive as it grows. Yarn, bond and affection reward effort only, and nothing lowers them.

Max level has two halves. Levels 1–5 grow with care: the protocols you do raise five attributes (Vitality, Rest, Calm, Focus and Spark). Levels 6–10 need your numbers to move. Each needs 28 days when a number sits ahead of where it started. Those days are never taken back.

The Max sheet opens from "Max your health" on the Health tab or from your cat's tab. Each attribute shows its care bar and your numbers, each beside your usual, with four weeks of movement in words. A number needs 10 days first.

Context for your age and sex is off until you turn it on. It covers cardio fitness, resting heart rate, steps and sleep, and names each study. It never covers heart rate variability. What you pick stays on your phone.

Going on an adventure

Caring for yourself fills the adventure bar. When it is full, send your cat out. An adventure takes about three hours; Home and Quests show when it will be back, and a notification says when it's home.

When it's home, open its story. After the very first adventure, each story stops once at a fork: pick one of two paths. Each path ends the story its own way, finds something different and teaches your cat a like or a dislike. Both bring the same yarn, so there's no wrong choice. On a first visit it may ask you a question too. Some trail places are part of Plus; the rest are free.

Your cat never sulks if you skip a day, and nothing about it can be lost.

Widgets, Apple Watch and the Dynamic Island
  • Widgets: add Kitty Max to your Home Screen or Lock Screen. You can tick steps right from a widget.
  • Apple Watch: your cat, your streak and Max level, today's steps to tick, a four-in, six-out breathing exercise and three complications.
  • Dynamic Island: a focus timer shows there while it runs.

To add a widget, long-press your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Kitty Max. A widget can lag a little behind the app.

Health and your baseline

Your scores and vitals

The Health tab has three parts: Today, Vitals and My Health.

Today shows three scores, each 0 to 100: Readiness, Sleep and Activity. Each one is built only from your own usual, never from other people's numbers, and each has a soft word under it:

  • Great: 85 and up
  • Good: 70 to 84
  • Fair: 60 to 69
  • Go gently: under 60

Tap a score to see what fed it. Each part reads On track, A little off, or Worth a look. Before 6 pm, Activity counts what you've done so far, never what's left. You can hide the numbers on any score's page; the words stay.

Vitals shows each measure against your usual range: resting heart rate, heart rate variability, breathing, wrist temperature and blood oxygen where your watch records them, sleep, steps, daylight and cardio fitness. A small check means within your usual. A soft amber dot means worth a look: a late night, a hard workout or a cold can all move it. Tap one for four weeks with your usual shaded.

My Health has your month at a glance, what your habits did, your experiments and your areas.

What is your baseline?

Your baseline is your own usual for a Health measure, from your last 28 days: the middle value and the range your middle half of days sits in. Kitty Max only ever compares you with you, never with "most people", and never gives you a grade.

  • Today is described in words first: about usual, a little or well above, a little or well below.
  • Before 10 days of data, your cat says it is still learning.
  • Your cat uses your baseline to tell when today is off, and how you're really doing.
Readiness

Readiness puts today's heart rate variability, resting heart rate and sleep against your baseline, as 0 to 100: Great, Good, Fair or Go gently. Tap it to see which part moved.

You can hide the score in Settings; the words still show. Readiness is not a medical measure.

Connecting Apple Health
  1. Tap Connect Apple Health in onboarding, or in Settings under Apple Health.
  2. In the sheet that opens, allow the kinds of data you're comfortable sharing.

Kitty Max reads sleep, steps, workouts, daylight, heart rate variability, resting and walking heart rate, wrist temperature and blood oxygen where a watch records them, and a few more. It uses them to tick steps for you, read last night's sleep, learn your baseline and fill in your scores and vitals. Your Health numbers stay on your phone.

You don't need Apple Health. Log how you slept in the morning log and your cat can still learn your baseline.

Experiments

An experiment is two weeks of one protocol, measured against your baseline. It ends with a verdict: a clear change, a slight change, no clear change, or not enough data. Your cat is glad of "no clear change" too; now you know.

Experiments open once any measure has a baseline, and by day 14 at the latest. An experiment series is a few experiments in a row toward one aim, like finding what moves your sleep. One experiment at a time is free; Plus runs them back to back.

Patterns, not proof

Kitty Max says what moved on the days you did something. It never says the habit caused it, because a two-week test with one person can't show that. That is why you see "a pattern, not proof".

See your week against your baseline under Patterns, from day 10.

Your areas and places

The Health tab has one page for each of the eight areas: what it is, your protocols and levels, your measures against your baseline, its experiment series and its place. A place grows as you run experiments in its area.

Kitty Max features

Streaks and rest days

Your streak counts days with any care. One missed day in seven is covered by a built-in rest day, shown with a moon. Your cat is never sad when you're away, and every finished day names a reason to come back tomorrow.

Quests and journeys
  • Daily quests: small tasks that reset every day. In your first weeks they are a tour of the app.
  • Journeys: a short story with one step a day. Each step tells a bit of your cat's story and finds something for its collection. Finishing one earns a keepsake. Journeys open on day 10; some are part of Plus.
  • Seasons: keepsakes that arrive with the time of year.
Calm and focus

Open Calm & focus from the Health tab. It has breathing patterns, focus timers, and first-aid tools for rough moments, from a panic reset to a body scan. They are always free.

Breathing is also on Apple Watch, and a running focus timer shows in the Dynamic Island.

The journal and Today I…

The journal is private and free. In the evening, "Today I…" lets you tick things you did, like a walk or time outside; those feed "what works for you" in Plus. Nothing you write leaves your phone, and friends never see it.

The Shop
  • Hundreds of pieces to buy with yarn: hats, neck, face and back pieces, room pieces, wallpapers, floors, and furniture your cat really uses.
  • Many pieces come in colours. Another colour of a piece you own is half price.
  • Tap a piece to see it on your cat first. The purchase card shows what's left after you pay; tap Collect to buy.
  • A gift box at the top opens once a day, and a trader visits each week.
  • Can't afford something yet? Tap "Save for this" and the Shop shows how far along you are.
  • Changed your mind? Long-press a piece you bought to sell it back for half what you paid.
  • After a week of care, a welcome bundle appears once: 250 yarn and one colour pick.

There are no timers, no random stock and no loot boxes, and everything can be earned with care's yarn.

Collections and tricks

The collection book lists eight collections. Own every piece in one (any colour of a piece counts) and your cat learns a trick, like a wave, a bow or a happy dance. It does them now and then at home. Tricks are earned, never sold.

The Micropet Lab

The Micropet Lab opens on day 15, in your cat's tab under Companions. It turns one goal you repeat into a small surprise.

  1. Open Companions, then Micropet Lab.
  2. Pick a goal that comes back, like a daily step. That goal now keeps an egg warm.
  3. Do the goal on seven different days. Twice in one day counts once, and undoing a tick takes the day back.
  4. On the seventh day the egg hatches into a side companion. You can bring it along right away.

What can hatch: eight common companions, four rare ones, and two seasonal rares each season that come back every year. About 1 egg in 6 is rare, and you can get the same one twice. One egg at a time. Nothing in the Lab costs yarn or money.

Your cat's tab and moments

Your cat's tab has its mood, its attributes and Max level, the wardrobe, the room and garden, companions, collections, and Friends.

Moments are the heart moments that come as your bond grows and the surprises that only happen once. They're kept here, so you can look back at them.

Notifications and reminders

Kitty Max can send:

  • a morning message that names that day's plan
  • a wind-down in the evening
  • a daylight nudge after you wake
  • journey steps and hugs from friends
  • a note when your cat is home from an adventure
  • the trial reminder

Choose times in Settings under Reminders. Turn any of them off in iOS Settings, Notifications, Kitty Max.

Glow: skin, hair, teeth and posture

Glow is the eighth area: the looks side of maxing, with the research behind each step.

  • Daily sunscreen, up to reapplying every two hours outdoors.
  • Cleanse and moisturise, morning and night.
  • Night retinoid: retinol, then adapalene, then prescription tretinoin with a dermatologist.
  • Azelaic acid (optional), for redness, bumps and uneven tone.
  • Vitamin C serum under sunscreen.
  • Minoxidil for thinning hair.
  • Floss and brush, and a posture reset.
  • A monthly skin check, using the ABCDE list.

Glow protocols with stronger ingredients carry cautions: read them before you start, and don't use retinoids or minoxidil if you're pregnant. Minoxidil is poisonous to cats, so wash your hands and keep the skin you put it on away from yours.

On the Glow page, rate your skin each day from 1 to 5. Experiments on skincare protocols compare that rating with your usual. With a watch that records it, the page also shows the day's UV and nudges you to reapply.

Friends

Adding friends

Friends open on day 14, in your cat's tab. Invite a friend with a link or a code. Friendships go both ways: adding or removing one shows on both phones.

Hugs, cheers and gifts
  • Hugs and cheers send your friend's cat a visit and a message.
  • Gifts can be yarn or a piece from the Shop, with daily limits so nobody farms.
  • Visit a friend's garden through the garden gate.
What your friends can see

Friends see your cat, its look and room, your levels and this week's streak. They never see your journal, your moods, your plan or your Health data. Friends use Apple's iCloud sharing, with no server of ours in between.

To stop, open a friend and choose Unfriend, or choose Leave Friends to leave entirely. Your own cat and progress aren't affected.

Plus, yarn and billing

What's free, and what Plus adds

Core care, the calm tools and the journal are free, for good. Plus is optional.

Plus adds: experiments back to back and what works for you, the Starlit collection, half as much yarn again plus 300 a month, long-range Health trends, more journeys and trail places, and deeper insights.

The monthly letter arrives in Mail on your first visit of a new month. It covers the month before: the days you cared, your typical numbers against the month before that, experiments finished, and days a number sat ahead of where it started.

Kitty Max never shows Plus unasked more than once a week, and never to someone who has it.

Plus pricing and the free trial
  • Monthly: $9.99
  • Yearly: $49.99
  • Lifetime: $99.99, paid once

Some yearly plans start with a 7-day free trial. A trial becomes a yearly subscription unless you cancel at least a day before it ends; the Plus screen shows the date, and you get a reminder before it. Prices can differ by country; the App Store shows what you'll pay before you confirm.

Buying yarn
  • Handful of yarn: 100 for $1.99
  • Basket of yarn: 300 for $4.99
  • Chest of yarn: 750 for $9.99
  • Big basket of yarn: 1,600 for $19.99

Open the Shop and tap your yarn, or tap Get yarn when a piece costs more than you have. Everything in the Shop can also be earned with care's yarn; packs only make it faster. Yarn packs can't be restored on a new phone, but the yarn you already have comes with you when iCloud sync is on.

Restoring or canceling Plus

Restore: on a new phone, open Plus (or Settings) and tap Restore purchases. Plus and lifetime come back.

Cancel: in iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, Kitty Max, Cancel Subscription. Plus stays on until the end of the time you paid for. Anything you collected stays yours.

Refunds and billing

Apple handles billing and refunds. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase. For anything else about a charge, email us; the address is under Help in the app.

Our approach

Our approach to self-care

Kitty Max is a small companion that helps you take small steps. Every habit starts tiny and levels up, and a day you miss costs you nothing.

  • Honest numbers. Always against your own baseline. Words first, number second. Never a grade.
  • No pushing. Your cat never asks you to tick something to make it happy.
  • Free at heart. Care, calm and the journal stay free.
Your cat's personality

Your cat is sneaky, warm and exact. It's always up to something small, it's on your side, and about your numbers it never jokes or guesses. It teases itself, never you. When you're struggling, it drops the act and sits with you.

It remembers what it said so lines don't repeat, and it asks you a question each day and brings your answer back later.

Where the habits come from

The protocols are general wellness habits drawn from published research, and each one lists its sources. A few, like sauna or cold exposure, carry cautions.

Kitty Max is general wellness, not medical advice or a medical device. Talk to a doctor about health concerns.

Your privacy

There's no Kitty Max account. Your cat, plan, journal and Health numbers live on your phone, and in your own private iCloud if you turn on iCloud sync. Kitty Max adds to anonymous weekly counts, like how many people finish onboarding: no name, no ID, nothing you wrote and nothing from Health. Turn that off in Settings.

Settings, Your data exports or deletes everything. The full privacy policy is in Settings too.

Something's wrong

My sleep or Health numbers are missing
  • Check that Apple Health is connected, and that sleep and the other kinds are allowed. In the Health app: your profile picture, Apps, Kitty Max.
  • Make sure your watch or tracker has synced to Apple Health.
  • No data for last night? Log it in the morning log and your cat can still learn.
  • "Still learning your baseline" is normal for the first 10 days.
Reminders aren't arriving
  1. Allow notifications for Kitty Max in iOS Settings.
  2. Check that a Focus isn't silencing them.
  3. Check your reminder times in Settings, Reminders.
  4. Open the app once after an update so it can plan them again.
My cat isn't on my new phone

Your progress lives on your phone as one file. iCloud sync keeps a copy in your own private iCloud.

  1. On your old phone, turn on iCloud sync in Settings and wait for it to say it's up to date.
  2. On the new phone, sign in with the same Apple ID, install Kitty Max and turn on iCloud sync.

Deleting the app without iCloud sync removes its data. There's no Kitty Max account to recover it from.

A purchase didn't arrive
  1. Check your connection and wait a minute.
  2. Tap Restore purchases.
  3. If a yarn pack was charged but the yarn is missing, report a problem with the date. Your Apple receipt email helps.
Friends can't find me
  • Friends need iCloud: check you're signed in to iCloud in iOS Settings.
  • Send your invite link again, or share your code.
  • Friends open on day 14; before then the Friends area isn't there yet.
Something looks broken

Sorry about that. Close Kitty Max fully and open it again; if it still looks wrong, report a problem. The email comes ready with your app version, phone model and a few settings, so we can find it faster. You can read and change all of it before you send.

Nothing matches that. Try a shorter word, or email us.
